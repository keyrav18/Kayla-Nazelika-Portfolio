About Me

Hello! I am a Bachelor’s degree graduate in Informatics Engineering from UIN Syarif Hidayatullah Jakarta (GPA 3.80), with a focus and experience in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Data Analysis, and application development. I have experience developing AI-based solutions such as KANA AI, an Al-Qur’an chatbot utilizing Large Language Models (LLM), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Knowledge Graph, and GraphRAG, as well as developing Computer Vision-based models.

I am proficient in Python, JavaScript, PHP, SQL, and MySQL, with experience in data processing and management using Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets. I also have experience in information system development, Web Development, UI/UX Design, IT Support, Software Testing, and Data Processing. Beyond my technical expertise, I have been actively involved in organizational activities and gained leadership experience as the Head of the Management Division. I have also worked as a Teaching Assistant and contributed to information system development projects. Additionally, I contributed as an editor to the development of an ISBN-registered Big Data textbook published by UIN Jakarta Press. I am a fast learner who is adaptable, analytical, detail-oriented, responsible, and capable of working independently as well as collaboratively within a team.
